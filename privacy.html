Privacy Policy – Rename Conversation

Last updated: 2026-11-20

What the add-on accesses

When you click Rename, the add-on reads the messages of the Gmail conversation you have open, rewrites their Subject line, saves the rewritten copies back into your own mailbox, and moves the originals to your Gmail Trash.

What it does not do

  • It does not send your email or any data derived from it to the developer or to any third party.
  • It does not store your email anywhere outside your own Gmail account.
  • It does not read conversations you have not opened and chosen to rename.
  • It does not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Where processing happens

All processing runs in Google Apps Script under your own Google account. Technical error messages may be written to Google Cloud logging for troubleshooting.

Revoking access

You can remove the add-on's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact
